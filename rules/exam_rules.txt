EXAMINATION RULES AND MALPRACTICE GUIDELINES

Rule 1: Students must not use mobile phones during examinations
unless explicitly authorized by the examination authority.

Rule 2: Suspected unauthorized electronic devices must be reviewed
by an authorized invigilator.

Rule 3: CCTV footage may be reviewed to investigate a suspected
examination rule violation.

Rule 4: A detected mobile phone is an alert for human review.
It is not, by itself, proof of malpractice.

Rule 5: Any incident report must include the timestamp, detection
confidence, relevant evidence, and reviewer observations.

Rule 6: Final decisions must be made by authorized examination
officials after reviewing the available evidence.
